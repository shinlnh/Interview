# Projects and Motivation

### 7.1 “What interests you most about working in a robotics/hardware concept?”

Các follow-up có thể gặp:

- Why robotics instead of pure AI/software?
- Why humanoid robots?
- What does “physical intelligence” mean to you?
- Which part interests you more: perception, planning, control or learning?
- What is the hardest difference between AI in simulation and AI on real hardware?
- How does your mathematics background help you in robotics?
- What do you want to learn next?

### 7.2 “Why are you interested in joining our company?”

Các ý em đã dùng trong form:

- Mục tiêu không chỉ làm humanoid mà muốn robot có hành vi/chuyển động gần con người hơn.
- Ý tưởng research recovery của em lấy cảm hứng từ cách con người xử lý lỗi.
- Em quan tâm đến hướng EVE / human-like dexterity and efficiency.
- Em thấy job post nhắc đến một **“AI engineer with a championship mindset.”**
- Em thấy hướng công ty gần với hướng em muốn theo đuổi.

### Follow-up

- What do you know about Physical Robotics?
- Why our company instead of another humanoid robotics company?
- What does human-like dexterity mean to you?
- Why is efficiency important in humanoid robots?
- What exactly about EVE interests you?
- How would your current skills contribute to our team?
- What do you want to learn here?
- Where do you see yourself in humanoid robotics in 3–5 years?
- What does a “championship mindset” mean to you?
- Give an example where you demonstrated that mindset.
